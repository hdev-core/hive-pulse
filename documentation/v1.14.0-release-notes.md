# HivePulse 1.14.0 — Release Notes

The Pulse feed learns to read the internal market, and frontend switching stops sending you to pages that don't exist.

## 📈 Your market activity now shows up in Pulse

Trading on Hive's internal market used to be invisible in HivePulse. It isn't any more — limit orders, fills, cancellations, expiries and HBD↔HIVE conversions all appear in the Pulse feed, with a dedicated **Market** tab so order activity doesn't bury your mentions and replies.

Rows say what actually happened: what you sold, what you received, and the rate you got. A cancellation shows the amount returned to you. An order the chain expired is labelled as expired, not as something you did.

If your recent history has no market activity, the Market tab now offers to search further back rather than claiming there's nothing there — an account with any real volume can easily have none in its most recent thousand operations.

## 🔗 Frontend switching goes to pages that exist

- **Ureka** posts open at the right URL, and its composer is `/create`. Switching to Ureka no longer carries across paths it doesn't serve.
- **Switching to a frontend from a page it has no equivalent of** now lands on that frontend's home page instead of a broken URL. Previously `peakd.com/trending/hive-167922` → Actifit produced a server error — and with auto-redirect enabled, you were sent there automatically.
- **A frontend you switch off in Settings is genuinely off.** The toggle used to turn green and change nothing, and disabled frontends could still be picked as your auto-redirect target.
- **Deleting a custom frontend** no longer leaves auto-redirect pointing at nothing, which previously misfired on every Hive page you opened.
- **Mixed-case links** like `peakd.com/@Alice/My-Post` are now recognised; they used to be ignored entirely.
- **The side panel keeps up.** It stays open while you browse, and now tracks the page you're actually on instead of the one it was opened on.

## ✍️ Post Analyzer

- **SlothBuzz tags are counted.** At SlothBuzz's ten-tag limit the analyzer read your tags as zero — precisely when you'd finished tagging properly.
- **The analyzer can be switched off** in Settings if you'd rather write without it. On by default.

## 🔍 Under the hood

- Sharper toolbar icon: the extension declared four icon sizes and shipped one image for all of them.
- Slow or failing Hive nodes now time out and fall back instead of leaving the feed spinning, and a failed page load no longer clears what you'd already loaded.
- Dropped a permission that let any website detect the extension, and two host permissions that were requested but never used.
- The privacy policy has been rewritten to describe what the extension actually reads and which services it contacts.
- First automated test suite: 71 tests covering URL routing, tag detection and market-operation parsing.
