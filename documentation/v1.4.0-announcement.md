# HivePulse v1.4.0: The Heartbeat of Hive Gets Stronger! ✨ Real-time Prices, User Notifications & More

Hello Hive Community! 🐝

We are thrilled to announce that **HivePulse v1.4.0** is now LIVE on the Chrome Web Store! This update is a major milestone in our mission to provide the ultimate "Heads-up Display" (HUD) for the Hive blockchain, making your daily interactions smoother, faster, and more informed.

From real-time market data to deep social integration, v1.4.0 brings the features you've been asking for.

---

## 💓 The Pulse: Real-time User Notifications
Stay on top of your social interactions without ever leaving your current tab. We’ve introduced a dedicated **User Notifications** view (The Pulse) right under your stats. 
- **Infinite Scroll:** Seamlessly browse through your mentions and replies with our new "load more" functionality.
- **Refined UI:** Mention notifications have been polished for better readability and a cleaner feel.

## 📈 Market Intelligence at a Glance
Keep your finger on the pulse of the Hive economy.
- **Header Price Tracker:** A real-time Hive price tracker is now integrated directly into the extension header.
- **Stats Page Upgrades:** The stats view now explicitly displays both Hive and HBD prices, so you're always aware of your account's value.

## 🔍 Find Anyone, Anywhere: Enhanced User Search
Finding and connecting with fellow Hivers has never been easier.
- **Visual Search:** User search now features **Hive avatars** and selection feedback.
- **Bad Actor Protection:** We've integrated the **Bad Actor List** directly into the search. Potential bad actors are now detected and highlighted while you search, helping you stay safe within the ecosystem.
- **Smart Routing:** Standardized frontend paths ensure that when you find a user, you can jump straight to their profile on your favorite interface.

## 🛠️ Your Pulse, Your Way: Customization & Ecosystem
We believe in the power of choice. HivePulse is now more customizable than ever.
- **Custom Frontends:** You can now add and manage your own custom frontends!
- **Frontend Management:** Reorder, activate, or deactivate frontends to match your personal workflow.
- **Badge Prioritization:** Choose what matters most to you—set your extension badge to prioritize either your unread message count or your VP/RC levels.
- **Expanded dApp List:** Discover the breadth of Hive with an expanded and categorized list of dApps, including new additions like **HiveScan** and updated branding for **Tribaldex** and **HiveStats**.

## 🎥 3Speak Integration
We are proud to welcome **3Speak** into the HivePulse family! Switcher support for 3Speak is now fully integrated, allowing you to jump from a post on any frontend directly to its video counterpart on 3Speak.

## 🛡️ Security & Safety First
Your safety is our priority. HivePulse v1.4.0 now integrates the **Bad Actor List** from Syncad GitLab. We've also added a comprehensive **Privacy Policy** to ensure full transparency on how we handle your data (locally and securely!).

---

### 🚀 Other Improvements & Polish
We've spent a lot of time "under the hood" to make HivePulse faster and more reliable:
- **Improved Login Experience:** A new top bar for a smoother sign-in flow.
- **Better Badge Logic:** Fixed delays in badge updates and prevented text cutoff.
- **Ecency Chat Enhancements:** Better support for dynamic loading in Ecency chat and notifications.
- **Surgical Bug Fixes:** From restoring the Actifit logo to fixing custom frontend detection, we’ve squashed the bugs that were slowing you down.
