# HivePulse v1.5.0: Your Hive Wallet, Now Supercharged! 💰 Hive-Engine Assets, Node Control & More

Hey Hiveans! 🐝

We are excited to announce that **HivePulse v1.5.0** is now LIVE on the Chrome Web Store! This update brings one of our most requested features to life — a true all-in-one wallet experience that unifies your Hive and Hive-Engine assets in one beautiful view. If v1.4.0 was about social pulse, v1.5.0 is about financial pulse.

---

## 💼 The New Wallet Tab: Everything You Own, In One Place
We have completely reimagined how you view your wealth on Hive.
- **Portfolio Value in USD:** Your total holdings are now calculated and displayed in real-time US Dollar value. No more mental math — see exactly what your stack is worth at a glance.
- **Comprehensive Asset Breakdown:** Dive deep into your portfolio with a detailed breakdown of every asset you hold.
- **Hive-Engine Integration:** Hive-Engine tokens are now fully integrated into your portfolio! Your HE holdings are fetched, priced, and displayed alongside your Hive and HBD.
- **Token Sorting & Filtering:** Easily sort and filter your Hive-Engine tokens to find exactly what you're looking for.
- **Live Token Logos & Market Prices:** Hive-Engine tokens now display their official logos and accurate market prices pulled directly from Hive-Engine market data.

## 🌐 Node Selector: Take Control of Your Connection
Sometimes the default path isn't the fastest. Now, you choose the route.
- **Dual Node Selection:** A brand new node selector lets you independently configure both your **Hive** and **Hive-Engine** RPC endpoints.
- **Custom Node Options:** Not seeing your preferred node in the list? You can add your own custom RPC endpoints for both Hive and Hive-Engine.
- **Tailored to You:** Prefer a specific Hive API node for account queries, but a different Hive-Engine node for token data? No problem — you can mix and match to build the fastest, most reliable connection for your workflow.
- **Bypass Slowdowns Instantly:** If a node is lagging or temporarily down, simply switch to another with a single tap. No more waiting on unresponsive endpoints!

## 🔧 Under the Hood: Hive-Engine Polished to Perfection
We know how important Hive-Engine is to the ecosystem, so we invested heavily in making the experience rock-solid.
- **Robust Token Fetching:** Completely rewritten token fetching logic with better error handling and retry mechanisms.
- **No More Loading Hangs:** Fixed edge cases where HE token icons or data could cause the view to hang.
- **Correct API Endpoints:** Updated to use the proper Hive-Engine API endpoints for reliable data.
- **Enhanced Logging:** Added comprehensive debugging capabilities to keep things running smoothly.

## 📊 UI/UX Refinements
- **Wallet-Focused Navigation:** The Apps tab has been replaced by the new Wallet tab, putting your assets front and center.
- **Dedicated Portfolio View:** The PortfolioCard has been moved to its own dedicated view for a cleaner, more focused experience.
