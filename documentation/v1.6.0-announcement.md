# HivePulse v1.6.0: Multi-Account, Full Wallet & Smarter Notifications! 🔔💼

Hey Hiveans! 🐝

We are thrilled to announce that **HivePulse v1.6.0** is now LIVE on the Chrome Web Store! This is our biggest update yet — one that transforms HivePulse from a handy companion into a true power-user cockpit for the Hive blockchain. If v1.5.0 gave you your wallet, v1.6.0 makes it yours across every account you manage.

## 👥 Multi-Account Support: Switch Instantly, Stay in Control

For the many of you juggling multiple Hive accounts — creators, curators, developers, and teams — this one is for you.

- **Account Switcher Dropdown:** A new account switcher lives right in the header. Tap it to add, select, or remove accounts without ever opening a new browser tab.
- **Per-Account Session Storage:** Each account stores its own session tokens independently. Switching accounts is instant — no re-login required as long as your session is still valid.
- **Add Account Modal:** Adding a second (or third!) account is a breeze. Just enter the username, authenticate via Keychain, and you are in.

---

## 💸 Wallet: Transact Directly from the Extension

Your wallet is no longer just a read-only dashboard. You can now act on your assets without ever leaving HivePulse.

- **Quick Send:** Transfer HIVE or HBD to any Hive account — complete with recipient validation (including debounced lookup), amount input, optional memo, and a Max button. Powered by Keychain, so your keys never touch us.
- **Quick Receive:** Instantly display your Hive address with a one-click copy button. Share it with anyone, anywhere.
- **Transfer History:** Browse your last 20 transfers with clear in/out indicators. Need to go further back? Hit "Load More" for cursor-based pagination through your full history.
- **Claim Rewards in One Click:** The portfolio pending section now features a **Claim All** button. When viewing your own account, claim your HIVE, HBD, and HP rewards in a single Keychain action — no frontend needed.

---

## 🏦 HBD Savings Dashboard: Put Your HBD to Work

HBD savings is one of Hive's most powerful — and underused — features. We have built a full dashboard around it.

- **Accrued Interest Estimate:** See how much interest your HBD savings have built up since your last payment, calculated in real time from chain data.
- **Claim Interest Button:** Trigger your interest payment directly from the extension. The button is intelligently gated on the 30-day compound interval, showing a countdown timer when you are not yet eligible.
- **Interest Payment History:** Review your last 5 interest payments at a glance.
- **Live APR Display:** The current HBD savings APR is fetched live from `condenser_api.get_dynamic_global_properties` and shown right alongside your balance.

---

## 💡 Earning Explainer: Understand Your Hive Income

New to Hive earnings, or just want a refresher? The **Earning Explainer** in the Wallet tab breaks down all four income streams with a clean, collapsible 4-card module:

- **Content Rewards** — author payouts from posts and comments
- **Curation Rewards** — rewards for voting early and well
- **Savings Interest** — passive yield on your HBD in savings
- **Governance** — returns from witness and DHF proposal voting

Each card includes the current live rate where applicable.

---

## 🔔 Notifications Tab: The Pulse, Fully Realised

The Stats tab has been completely rebuilt as a dedicated **Notifications** hub — no more cluttered gauges and search boxes, just the information that matters.

- **Unified Feed with Smart Filtering:** The Pulse now has four filter tabs — **All**, **Social**, **Finance**, and **Votes** — each showing exactly the notifications you care about, grouped by recency (Today, Yesterday, This Week, This Month, Older).
- **Full Finance History:** We now pull from `condenser_api.get_account_history` in addition to the bridge API, surfacing notifications that were previously invisible:
  - Interest payments
  - Reward claims
  - Power-ups and Power-downs
  - Savings deposits, withdrawals, and fills
  - Proposal payments
- **Background Notification Polling:** The extension now silently checks for new Hive notifications in the background. When something arrives that you haven't seen, a **red badge** appears on the extension icon with the unread count.
- **Configurable Badge & Filters:** In Settings, you can toggle the notification badge on or off and choose exactly which notification types trigger it. Votes are off by default to keep things focused.
- **Finance Pagination Fixed:** "Load Older" in the Finance tab now correctly pages through your account history using a sequence-number cursor — it was silently broken before.
- **Accurate Counts:** The total count shown next to "The Pulse" now reflects the true combined total from both data sources, capped at 99+.

## 🌱 Onboarding & UX Polish

- **First-Run Banner:** New users are greeted with a friendly, dismissible onboarding banner that introduces key features on their very first open.
- **Contextual Tooltips:** Hover over VP, RC, HIVE, HBD, and all portfolio asset labels to get a quick explanation. Tooltips are viewport-aware — they never get clipped by the extension boundaries.
- **Version Number in Settings:** The current version ("HivePulse v1.6.0") is now shown at the bottom of the Settings screen, so you always know exactly what you are running.
