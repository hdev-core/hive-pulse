## ⚡ The Wallet Grows Up: From Read-Only to a Full Action Center

This is the heart of the release. Until now the wallet *showed* you your Hive — now it lets you **manage all of it**, right from the popup, with every transaction signed locally through **Hive Keychain**. Nothing leaves the page, no keys are ever stored.

What you can now do without leaving HivePulse:

- **💸 Send & receive** HIVE and HBD, with live recipient validation, a memo field, and a built-in **transfer history** tab.
- **🎁 Claim rewards** in one tap — pending HIVE, HBD, and HP — plus a one-click **HBD savings interest** claim when it's due.
- **🔼 Power Up / 🔽 Power Down** your Hive Power, with a clear 13-week breakdown and a **Stop power-down** button if you change your mind.
- **🤝 Delegate HP** to any account (and remove a delegation just as easily) — with recipient validation and automatic HP→VESTS conversion.
- **🐷 Savings** — move HIVE or HBD into savings and request withdrawals, right alongside the live APR view.

And because actions should be where you expect them, every asset row now has **quick-action pills** — tap **Send**, **Power Up**, **Power Down**, **Delegate**, **Stake**, or **Unstake** on a token and it jumps straight to the right form with everything pre-filled.

## 🎮 Full Hive-Engine Token Control

Your Hive-Engine tokens were already visible in the portfolio — now they're **actionable**. Pick any token you hold and:

- **Send** it to another account (with a memo),
- **Stake** it, or
- **Unstake** it.

HivePulse is smart about it, too: **stake/unstake only appear for tokens that actually support staking** (so you'll never see them on SWAP.HIVE, DEC, or SWAP.HBD), quantities are formatted to each token's real precision, and your balances refresh automatically after every action.

## ⏳ Unstaking in Progress: See It and Cancel It

Ever start a power-down or a token unstake and forget about it? There's now a dedicated **"Unstaking in Progress"** panel that surfaces:

- your active **Hive Power down** — how much is left, the weekly rate, weeks remaining, and the next payout date, and
- any in-flight **Hive-Engine token unstakes** — amount remaining, payouts left, and next release.

Each one comes with a **one-tap cancel** (Stop the power-down, or cancel an HE unstake to return the tokens to your stake). No more digging through a block explorer to find out what's still cooking.

## ✨ Introducing the "For You" Feed

The old **Trending** tab is now **Feed**, and it opens on a brand-new **✨ For You** view: a personalized stream of Hive posts chosen for *you* — not just whatever is globally trending.

### What it does

Every other feed on Hive shows everyone the same thing. **For You is different for each person.** It surfaces posts that match *your* interests and the communities you actually care about, so what you see reflects how you really use Hive — a genuine, personalized discovery feed for the chain.

### How it works

For You is powered by **HAF FYP ("For You Page")**, a ranking service built on the **Hive Application Framework** — the same on-chain indexing layer that powers tools like Hivemind and block explorers. In plain terms:

1. **It learns your taste from the chain.** From your *public* on-chain history — the posts you write, vote on, and comment on, and the communities you subscribe to and engage with — it builds a lightweight **interest profile**: a vector of the topics you gravitate toward plus the communities you're most active in. Nothing private is involved; it only reads what's already public on Hive.
2. **It scores every candidate post** against that profile using a transparent, weighted blend of signals:
   - **🎯 Relevance** — how closely the post matches your interest profile *(the heaviest weight)*.
   - **🕒 Recency** — fresher posts rank higher, so the feed stays current.
   - **💬 Engagement** — how much the wider community is interacting with it.
   - **🛡️ Credibility** — a trust signal derived from the author, to keep quality up.
   - **🏘️ Community boost** — a lift for posts from communities you've **subscribed to** (full boost) or simply **engage with** (a smaller, "tiered" boost).
3. **It tells you *why*.** Each post carries a small chip explaining what surfaced it — a community you follow, or how strongly it matched your interests — so the ranking is never a black box.

Logged in, you get a feed ranked for *your* account. Logged out — or before the ranker has finished building your profile — you get a great **global** version of the same feed, so it's useful from the very first open. And because the scoring algorithm is **versioned**, it can be tuned and improved over time without you changing a thing.

> **⚙️ Heads-up — this is early-stage tech.** The HAF FYP service currently runs on the **`develop` branch**, served from a **test/development HAF node — it is *not* yet deployed on production HAF API nodes.** That means it's evolving quickly and may occasionally be unavailable while the node is updated or reindexed. When that happens, HivePulse automatically falls back to the global / Trending feeds, so the **Feed** tab always works. We're sharing it now because we're genuinely excited about what personalized, on-chain discovery can do for Hive — and we'd love your feedback as it matures toward production.

And the classics are all still here: **Trending**, **Hot**, **New**, and the **Communities** tab — browse however you like. As always, tapping a post opens it in *your* preferred frontend.

## 💬 Ecency Chat Is Back

Ecency recently changed how chat authenticates, which had left the in-extension chat unable to connect. We dug into it and **restored full chat functionality** — sign in once with Keychain and your channels, DMs, reactions, and unread badges all work again. We also smoothed out two rough edges: **long messages and links now wrap neatly** inside the bubble, and the **account switcher no longer hides behind an open conversation**.

## 🔧 Fixes & Polish

- **SWAP.HIVE now shows its real value.** It was being priced at $0 (it's the Hive-Engine base currency with no market against itself); it's now correctly valued 1:1 with HIVE — which also corrects your Hive-Engine total.
- **Staked Hive-Engine tokens are now shown and counted.** Tokens you hold purely as stake were previously invisible; the list now displays staked amounts and includes them in each token's value and your total.
- **Side-panel signing fixed.** Keychain actions taken from the browser **side panel** (on sites outside Hive) now sign reliably instead of erroring.
- Smarter session handling so the wallet and chat refresh themselves after you take an action.
