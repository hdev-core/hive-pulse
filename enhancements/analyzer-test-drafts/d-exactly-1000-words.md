This draft exists to test the word-count bar in the HivePulse post analyzer, and nothing else. It is built to land on exactly one thousand words as the analyzer counts them, so the bar has a number you can check it against rather than a feeling.

## What to look for

The words pill should read 1,000 exactly. The filled part of the bar should stop directly beneath the 1k mark on the scale underneath it, not short of it. Before this was fixed the numbers were spaced evenly across the track, so 1k sat at the halfway point and a thousand words looked like roughly eight hundred.

A post that is long enough to be useful is easier to rank than a short one. Subheadings give a reader somewhere to stop and a search engine a sense of structure. Short sentences read faster than long ones and they score better for reading ease. Connectors such as however and for example hold two ideas together in a way readers follow. An image every few hundred words keeps a long piece from becoming a wall of text.

The first tag on a Hive post is permanent, so it deserves a moment of thought. Writing for a person first and an algorithm second tends to satisfy both of them. A citation makes a claim checkable, which is the whole reason it adds trust. Linking one of your own earlier posts keeps a curious reader on chain a little longer. Numbers in a title tell a reader exactly how much of their time the piece will take.
