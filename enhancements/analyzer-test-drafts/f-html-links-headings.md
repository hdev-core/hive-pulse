This draft exists to test one thing: whether the analyzer reads a link and a subheading the same way whichever editor wrote them. PeakD's toolbar emits raw HTML. Ecency's emits markdown. The article is identical either way, so the score should be too.

<h2>Why links matter</h2>

An internal link to another Hive post keeps a reader on chain and builds topical authority around your account. An external link to a source adds trust, because a claim with a citation behind it is easier for a reader and a ranking system to believe. The analyzer wants at least one of each.

<h3>And subheadings</h3>

Subheadings do two jobs at once. They give a reader somewhere to stop in a long piece, and they give a search engine a map of what the article covers. One section every couple of hundred words is a reasonable rhythm for a post of this length.

<h2>Heading levels</h2>

The title of a Hive post is already the page H1. A heading at level one inside the body therefore creates a second H1, which weakens the structure rather than strengthening it. Start your body headings at level two and never skip a level on the way down.
