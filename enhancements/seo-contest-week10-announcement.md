### Three entries scored 100 and won nothing

@mayt and @les90 both published a perfect 200/200 post. @nursejoe scored 97/100. None of the three carried `#hivepulse`, so none of them could be ranked.

@nursejoe came closest of anyone this contest has seen: the tag was typed as **`hive pulse`, with a space**. A Hive tag cannot contain a space, so it never became `#hivepulse` at all. One keystroke.

We check the tag by hand on every entry, against the post's on-chain metadata and its body text, before anything is ranked. We are naming these three plainly rather than quietly dropping them, because the fix is free and takes two seconds, and because a rule nobody sees enforced is not a rule.

Three rounds ago every single entrant tagged correctly. It can be done.

### The tiebreak rule earned its keep again

@ahmedabbaci, @nabbas0786 and @wardah all finished on a perfect 200/200. The published tiebreak settled the podium with no judgement call: GEO first, which was 100 for all three, then word count — 3,332, then 2,326, then 1,295.

That is the second round running where the rule decided the podium instead of an opinion. It is published before each round for exactly this reason, and it will not change mid-round.
