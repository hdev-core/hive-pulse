## How were the HivePulse SEO Contest winners chosen?

No screenshot was taken on trust. Every entry was re-scored from its published on-chain body using the same scoring engine the extension runs in the editor, so a flattering screenshot could not win anything. The judging script is public inside the HivePulse repository, and the ranking metric was the combined average of the SEO percentage and the GEO score.

Three checks decided eligibility: publication inside the contest window, a verified on-chain author, and a minimum SEO score of 70. Merit did the rest.

### What separated the winners from everyone else?

The data answers this cleanly. SEO scores were strong across the board, averaging 89% — Hive creators already write good titles, use headings, and add images. GEO averaged 83 and swung from 60 all the way to 100, and **all three winners scored a perfect 100 on GEO**.

GEO is the measure of whether an AI answer engine can lift a passage from a post and cite it. Three habits produced every perfect score:

1. **Open with a summary.** State the payoff in the first 8 to 60 words, before any preamble.
2. **Write self-contained sentences.** A sentence beginning with "This shows..." means nothing once quoted alone. Name the subject instead.
3. **Keep pronoun density low.** Repeat the actual noun where a lesser draft would reach for "it" or "they".

Search engine optimization gets a post ranked. Generative engine optimization gets a post *quoted* — and quoting is how ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI overviews send readers somewhere new. Most entrants were already winning the first battle and losing the second.

## 🛠️ You reported, we shipped

Every substantive piece of feedback on the launch post turned into a released fix within days. **Version 1.12.0 is rolling out now**, and most of what follows came straight from this contest's comment section.

- **📱 Mobile support arrived.** @nabbas0786 pointed out that desktop-only shuts out a large share of Hive. **Firefox for Android is now supported** for reading and analyzing.
- **🌍 Non-English editors fixed.** @mein-senf-dazu and @militadigital01 both hit a description field that refused to register. The analyzer was only matching the English label; **German and Dutch preview descriptions are now captured correctly**.
- **🦥 More frontends supported.** @slothlydoesit made the case that frontends compete with their own content for search visibility. **SlothBuzz and Ureka are now supported**, with the SEO analyzer running on SlothBuzz.
- **🌐 Opera joins the lineup.** HivePulse already runs on Opera and other Chromium browsers through the Chrome build, and a dedicated Opera add-ons listing has been submitted for review, so Opera users will soon have a one-click install of their own.
- **🔧 Detection hardening.** Tag, heading, link and image detection are sharper across rich-text editors, so the score reflects the real post rather than what the editor happens to expose.
- **🐞 And a round of bug fixes.** Hover cards were quietly showing a reputation of **25 for everyone** — Hive deprecated the old reputation field and it now returns zero, so HivePulse reads hivemind's real value instead. Actifit links written without an `@` now route correctly to other frontends. The error that used to appear right after the extension updated itself is gone. And the analyzer panel now tells you which version you are running.
