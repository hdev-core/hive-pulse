## Where the points actually are

Three rounds of data now say the same thing. SEO scores cluster high — Hive creators already write good titles, headings and images. GEO is the metric that turns a good post into a citable one, and this week the field caught on: six of seven qualifying entries scored GEO 93+.

GEO measures whether an AI answer engine can lift a passage from your post and cite it. Three habits carry almost all of it:

1. **Open with the answer.** Put the payoff in the first 8 to 60 words, before any preamble.
2. **Write self-contained sentences.** A sentence beginning "This shows…" means nothing quoted alone. Name the subject.
3. **Keep pronoun density low.** Repeat the noun where a weaker draft reaches for "it" or "they".

And the cheapest points on the board: **fill in the preview description** — worth 10, takes 15 seconds. On PeakD it is "Short preview description", right under the editor. On Ecency it is in the Story preview step, the box under the greyed-out title — pre-filled from your post, so it looks like your text, but editing it changes only your search snippet.

And while we're here: **tag the post `#hivepulse`.** Not after you remember — at the top of the tag list, before you publish. It is the difference between the podium and a footnote.
