### We got the podium wrong, and we have paid to fix it

This part is about us, not about any of you.

Our judge was rounding the combined score before ranking. @nabbas0786 scored SEO 99 and GEO 100, which averages to **99.5** - and the rounding turned that into 100, made it look like a tie with @nursejoe's genuine 100, and the reading-ease tiebreak then handed him third place. A tiebreak ran on a tie that did not exist.

We found it after the round had closed, fixed it, and that moved third place to @nursejoe. **Applying it to a finished round was the wrong call.** Our own rule, published in this series, says *anything that changes is announced before the round it applies to, never during*. We changed how scores are compared five hours after the round closed, and it moved 50 HIVE.

So we have done both things. @nursejoe takes third on the corrected arithmetic, and **@nabbas0786 has been sent 50 HIVE separately**, restoring what the rule as it stood when he entered would have given him. Nobody is worse off under either reading, and no other placing moved. The round cost 400 HIVE instead of 350, and that difference is our error, not your prize pool.

**The fix itself is right, and it applies from week 15 onward** - announced here, in advance, as it should have been the first time. From now on the combined score is compared exactly: 99.5 is not a tie with 100.

## Week 15 is open - and the rewards change today

**Week 15 runs from 3 November to 10 November 2026, 12:00 UTC.**

Everything below applies to **this round**, starting now. You are reading it before the round you will enter, which is how any change here is supposed to work - and it asks something of you.

### Why we are changing it

Honest numbers from our own records. **In the last four rounds this contest has gained one new entrant.** Weeks 11, 12 and 13 brought in nobody at all. The field went from nine people down to three and back to five, and the people entering are almost entirely the same handful who have been here since the summer.

That is not a prize problem. Everyone who enters is already scoring well - in fourteen rounds only two entries have ever failed the qualifying bar, and both were in the first fortnight. It is a **discovery** problem. Most Hive writers have never heard of this contest.

So from this round the pool depends on the size of the field, in both directions.

### The new reward structure

| Valid entrants | Prize pool | Places paid |
|---|---:|---|
| Fewer than 5 | **Nothing - the pool rolls into the next round** | - |
| 5 - 9 | 300 HIVE | 150 · 100 · 50 |
| 10 - 14 | 400 HIVE | 200 · 100 · 60 · 40 |
| 15 - 19 | 500 HIVE | 250 · 120 · 80 · 50 |
| 20 or more | 600 HIVE | 250 · 150 · 100 · 60 · 40 |

The bigger the field, the more of you get paid - five places at twenty entrants, three at five.

**Below five entrants, no prizes are paid and the whole pool carries forward.** A round with four people pays nobody, and the following round is worth 600 instead of 300. Two quiet rounds running and it is still 600 - we cap the rollover there so the number stays believable.

We know exactly what this risks: **three of the last four rounds would have paid nothing under it.** This round could be one of them. We are doing it anyway, because a contest that quietly pays the same five people forever is not growing anything, and because a rollover turns a quiet week into a bigger prize rather than a dead loss.

Five people entered last round. **One more and this round pays.**

**What does not change on a quiet round:** every qualifying entry is still scored, still listed in the results, and still gets an upvote and a reblog from @hdev. You keep the scoring, the feedback and the reach. Only the prize money waits a week.

If you want the pool to grow, the lever is in your hands: bring someone. The referral bonus pays **25 HIVE to each of you** when the person you brought enters and qualifies.

### Ties will split the prize, instead of racing the clock

Today, when two entries tie, the podium goes to whoever published first. That has decided the top of the board for four rounds running, and it is a bad rule - it rewards rushing a post out on day one and punishes anyone who drafts, re-scores and improves before publishing, which is the entire point of the tool.

**Tied entries now share the prize slots they span, equally.**

| If this happens | Each gets |
|---|---|
| 2 tie for 1st | **125 and 125**, and the next entrant takes 3rd for 50 |
| 3 tie for 1st | 100 each |
| 4 tie for 1st | 75 each - yes, four people paid |
| Clear 1st, 2 tie for 2nd | 150, then 75 each |
| Clear 1st and 2nd, 2 tie for 3rd | 150, 100, then 25 each |

The total leaving the pot is always the same. Ties change how many of you get paid, not how much is paid. And the race to publish first simply disappears, because there is nothing left for it to decide. **Take the week. Draft it, score it, fix it, then publish.**

### How to enter

1. Install [HivePulse for Chrome](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/hivepulse/hakcpohpejoejmlhiphpkjobpjeckdlg) or [Firefox](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/hivepulse/).
2. Write and publish a new post on any supported Hive frontend with the analyzer open.
3. **Publish it inside the round window** - between the two dates above. A post written before the round opened cannot be entered.
4. Comment below with the post link and a screenshot of the SEO and GEO panel.
5. **Tag the post `#hivepulse`** - one word, no space, in the tags and not only in the body. Check the tag list before you hit publish. This is the most common way people lose an entry.
6. You may enter more than one post. Your highest-scoring tagged post counts.
7. If someone referred you, say so in your entry comment and name them. When the entry qualifies, both people receive 25 HIVE. The referred person must be new to the contest, with an account at least 30 days old.

The minimum qualifying score is **SEO 70**. Any language is welcome. Every entry is re-scored on-chain after the round closes.

**Ties now split the prize, so Reading Ease is only needed to separate entries that are genuinely NOT tied.** One definition, since it has decided podiums before: **Reading Ease is the whole number HivePulse shows you** - the same figure the SEO readability check scores against. The combined score is different: it comes from a published formula, `(SEO% + GEO)/2`, and from this round we compare it exactly.

### One more thing, because someone asked

A regular entrant asked us recently whether this contest exists for the community or just to test the extension, and whether HivePulse will still be here in a year. It was a fair question, we had never answered it anywhere, so here it is.

**HivePulse is a free browser extension** that scores a Hive post for Google and for AI assistants before you publish it. No paywall, no token, nothing to buy.

**We run this contest for two reasons, and both are true.** Hive's writing is close to invisible to Google and to ChatGPT, and we want more of it optimised. And every round tells us where our own scoring is wrong - the GEO rubric was rewritten because entries showed 57% of them maxing a check meant to be hard, the reading-ease tiebreak exists because of a three-way tie in week 11, and the ranking bug at the top of this post was found because five of you entered and three of you tied. You are not test subjects. You are the reason the thing works.

**It is community and it is testing. Those were never in tension.**
