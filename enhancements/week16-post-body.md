### What this is, in case you are new here

**HivePulse is a free browser extension** that scores a Hive post for Google and for AI assistants before you publish it. No paywall, no token, nothing to buy.

**We run this contest for two reasons, both true.** Hive's writing is close to invisible to Google and to ChatGPT, and we want more of it optimised. And every round tells us where our own scoring is wrong - the GEO rubric was rewritten because contest entries showed 57% of them maxing a check meant to be hard, and a ranking bug that had put third place with the wrong person was found because five of you entered and three tied.

This round, two of our own tools disagreed with the rules they enforce. One of them counted a notification bot as an entrant, which would have paid out a round that should not have paid. We found both, fixed both, and are telling you because you would want to know.
