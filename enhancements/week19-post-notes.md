Read on judging day (1 December 2026), per step 7 of the routine in `AGENTS.md`.

## 1. The week 17 correction — MUST be stated openly

The week 17 post told entrants, in its rules:

> **Reading Ease is the whole number HivePulse shows you,** and from today it is the same
> number your published post receives.

That was false for anyone writing on 17 November. The post went live at 15:21 UTC; 1.18.0,
which carried the new readability rule, was committed at 18:21 and approved on AMO at 18:36.
No browser had it, and the post never said an update was needed.

- **@itx.wardah** published 17:29 UTC, **@fluer** 19:07 UTC. Both panels ran 1.17.0 and
  showed ease 60 / SEO 100. The judge used 1.18.0 and gave them 99.5, off the podium.
- On the engine they actually had, all five tied at 100, which pays 60 each.
- **Paid: 60 HIVE each to @itx.wardah and @fluer**, 120 total, as a discretionary
  correction. Placings stand. Nobody gave anything back.
- Wardah reported it; she had checked and reloaded two or three times before posting.

**Why it has to be in the post:** the promise was made in public, so the correction has to
be public too. A correction that lives only in two transfer memos is invisible to the other
entrants, and to anyone deciding whether to trust what the next post says.

### Draft section

```markdown
### A correction from week 17

Our week 17 post said the Reading Ease HivePulse shows you is the number your published post receives, "from today". On the first day that was not true: the update carrying the new rule reached the stores a few hours after the post went live.

@itx.wardah and @fluer both posted that afternoon. Their installed version scored them 100; the judge used the new one and gave them 99.5. On their version all five entries tied at 100, which pays 60 each, so **we paid each of them 60 HIVE**. The week 17 placings stand.

From now on a scoring change starts only once the update is live in both stores, and the post announcing it will tell you to update.
```
