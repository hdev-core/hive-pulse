### Sat 22 Aug, 14:00 — kill the excuse
*Image: none*

> "My posts are too short to rank."
>
> Our perfect AI-quotability scores range from 624 words to 2,928.
>
> Length correlates with the score at 0.57 — real, but weak. A tight 600-word post that opens with its answer beats a rambling 2,000-word one. Structure is doing the work.

### Sun 23 Aug, 14:00 — the 30-second audit (teaching, most saveable)

> A 30-second audit you can run on anything you've published:
>
> 1. Read your first sentence alone. Does it answer the title, or clear its throat?
> 2. Find any sentence starting with "This", "It" or "They". Read it with nothing before it. Still meaningful?
> 3. Count how often you wrote "it" where the actual noun would fit.
>
> That is essentially the whole AI-quotability score. No tool required.
